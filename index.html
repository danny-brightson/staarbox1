Why Us?

Health Bites

Wellness isn’t just what you eat; it’s what you know. Simple tips on fruit, nuts, seasonal eating and daily routines, from fitness myths to food facts, to help you make smarter choices every day.

Gym & Fitness Goals

Pushing your limits at the gym? Staarbox brings protein-rich nuts and fresh fruits that keep you full and fuelled, with no artificial supplements. It’s clean, natural fuel your body will thank you for.

Watching Your Sugar

Watching your sugar? Our boxes are built around fresh whole fruits and unsweetened nuts, with natural fibre and good fats. If you have diabetes, please ask your doctor which fruits suit you.

For Mums-to-Be

Pregnancy is a time to eat well. Our boxes bring fresh fruits and nuts with natural folate, iron and fibre: a wholesome, natural start to the day. Every pregnancy is different, so please check with your doctor about what suits you.

Every Day of the Month

Your body’s needs change through the month. Our boxes include fruits and nuts with natural magnesium, potassium and good fats: a fresh, wholesome snack for every day of the month.
